# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-09-05

Initial research release: original inequality through excess 15, exact first auxiliary failure at excess 16, two auxiliary conjecture counterexamples, and unbounded obstruction families. Includes English and Chinese papers, the initial prompt, Lean source, verification evidence, and reproduction instructions. The unrestricted Erdős problem remains open.

The separately checked original excess-16 extension is reserved for a subsequent release after independent integration review; it does not change the sharp auxiliary threshold.
