# Beyond the Slack Barrier — v0.1.0

A bilingual research release with Lean-verified results around Erdős problem 488.

- The original strict density inequality holds for every nonempty primitive generator set with excess at most **15**, and for every later integer endpoint.
- The first possible failure of the auxiliary incidence-plus-cardinality bound is exactly **16**.
- Explicit finite witnesses refute Conjectures **4.8** and **6.11** in the inspected 20 March 2026 manuscript by Przemysław Chojecki.
- Further Lean theorems establish unbounded pair-tail ratios and auxiliary failures under simultaneous sparsity and coprimality constraints.

**The unrestricted Erdős problem remains open.** These auxiliary counterexamples are not counterexamples to the original problem. Global historical priority for all strengthened formulations has not been established.

## Included

English and Chinese papers (PDF, Markdown and LaTeX), the original research prompt and translation, Lean sources, dependency and hash manifests, actual verification records, finite-search evidence, exact figures, and English social cards.

The proofs use Lean **4.33.1**, pinned Mathlib, and trust level **0**. The included CI rebuilds three small Init-only certificates and checks the complete published source manifest. It does not represent a complete remote rebuild of every large certificate; the archived local records state their actual scope. Full reproduction is documented in the README.

The workflow began from a broad user prompt and proceeded through multiple continuation messages and AI-agent audits. At least **21,600.003879 seconds** of active search were recorded. AI-agent review is distinct from external peer review.
